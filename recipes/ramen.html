Ramen

Ramen is a staple in many Asian cultures and is widely loved and adopted throughout various cultures and societies. Making ramen can be simple or complex but the following recipe will be a fairly simple recipe to get you into it!

Ingredients

  • Ramen noodles (such as Maruchan or Shin Ramen
  • Garlic and ginger
  • Broth maybe chicken or veg
  • Dried shiitake mushrooms
  • Veggies like carrots or kale
  • All your favorite toppings like some panko, egg, chili oil, furukake, etc

Steps

  1. Stir-Fry the Aromatics Garlic and ginger, what a delicious duo. This is where the flavor is, friends.
  2. Make Your Broth Add some chicken broth and dried shitake mushrooms for some umami punch
  3. Add Noodles Cook your noodles right in the broth with some scallions (more flavor please)
  4. Add Veg Thinly sliced kale, shredded carrots, whatever you'd like! Cook until just tender
  5. Top it Off Add some cruchy panko crumbs, a soft-boiled egg, chili oil, hot sauce, sesame oil, and/or soy sauce, whatever your heart desires
